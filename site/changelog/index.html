0.1.0

in progress
  • LinySync starts from AnySync's engine and window with Linux on both ends: only Linux machines are listed and accepted, of any distro.
  • Its own window, shaped like a circuit board with corners cut at 45 degrees, in an amber palette with the LYSY logo.
  • Each machine gets an id, a name, a look with its distro on a badge, and a key pair.
  • A private GitHub repo as the bus: devices, requests and transfers, with anything private sealed.
  • The item catalogue under the home folder, and lists made with apt, dnf, pacman, Flatpak, Snap, Homebrew, npm, cargo, pipx and the editors.
  • The encrypted machine to machine stream, resuming after a cut, and GOAL.md for your agent.
  • CI tests and builds on Linux; tagging a version releases linysync-linux-x64.tar.gz.
